What is Wheel Out?

Wheel Out is an instant-win crash game by InOut Games. A tire launches and rolls across a desert while a multiplier climbs the longer it keeps going. You place a bet before the round, then cash out at any point to lock in your stake multiplied by the current value. If the tire crashes before you cash out, the bet is lost. The tension is entirely in the timing of the cash-out. Its published headline numbers are a 97% RTP, high volatility, and a maximum win of up to 10,000x your stake.

How to play Wheel Out

1) Set your bet before the round starts. 2) The tire launches and rolls across the desert as the multiplier rises. 3) Tap cash out at any moment to lock in your stake times the current multiplier. 4) If the tire crashes before you cash out, that bet is lost. There is no skill in the outcome itself, a certified RNG decides each crash point, so the only decision is when to take the money.

Stakes typically run from about $0.12 to $200 a round (roughly ₹10 to ₹16,600), though the exact minimum and maximum are set by the operator you play at and can differ from one casino to another. Check the bet limits shown in the game panel before your first round.

Wheel Out RTP and volatility

Wheel Out is published with a 97% RTP and high volatility. RTP (return to player) is a long-run statistical average measured over millions of rounds, it is not a promise about any single session. A 97% RTP does not mean you get 97% of every stake back; short sessions can swing far above or below it, and high volatility widens that swing. No result is ever guaranteed. High volatility means wins can be larger but come less often, so bankroll management matters. For the full breakdown, see our Wheel Out review.
