Wheel Out RTP Explained: 97% Over What Sample?

The 97% figure is a property of the game, not of your evening. Here is how to read it, how to verify it, and what it costs per thousand rounds.

By Ryan Murton · Crash games analyst 21 August 2026

🔞 18+ only · Real-money gambling

Every Wheel Out listing repeats the same number: 97% RTP. It is accurate, it is published by InOut Games, and it says nothing at all about how tonight goes.

Reading the figure correctly

RTP is total returns divided by total wagers across the game's full modelled history. The remaining 3% is the house edge — the price of playing, charged against everything you stake, not against what you lose.

Three common misreadings

  • “I get 97% of my deposit back.” No — the edge applies to turnover, and turnover recycles winnings.

  • “The game owes me after a bad run.” No. Rounds are independent; nothing is owed.

  • “Higher stakes reach the RTP faster.” No — they reach the expected cost faster.

Verifying it on an operator

  1. Open the in-game info panel and read the RTP stated there.

  2. Compare it with the figure published by InOut Games for the title.

  3. If they differ, the operator is running a lower-return configuration — that is legal on some titles and worth avoiding.

  4. Check the licence on the operator's footer while you are there.
